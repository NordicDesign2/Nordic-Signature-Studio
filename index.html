Add it to Gmail

Click Copy signature above — this copies the formatted signature, not just the text.

In Gmail, open Settings (gear icon) → See all settings → stay on the General tab.

Scroll to Signature, click Create new, and name it (e.g. your own name).

Click into the empty signature box and paste (Ctrl/Cmd + V). The logo, colours and layout should appear exactly as in the preview.

Set it as your default for new emails and replies, scroll down, and click Save Changes.

The signature uses Arial as its font rather than our brand typefaces (Atyp BL / Open Sans) — most email clients, Gmail included, strip custom web fonts from pasted signatures and fall back to a system font regardless, so Arial keeps it looking the same for everyone who receives your email. If your paste ever comes through as plain, unstyled text, use a Chromium-based browser (Chrome or Edge) — Gmail's signature box reads formatting most reliably from those.

If you've ticked on an event banner, it copies in underneath the signature block automatically — no extra step needed in Gmail.
